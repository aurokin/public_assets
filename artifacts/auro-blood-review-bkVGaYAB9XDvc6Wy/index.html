02

Trash → boss → next burst

Give the next DRW a job.

On the last trash pack, choose whether burst belongs here or on the coming boss or add wave. Trash DRW has value. A late first boss use is not automatically a ready-button error.

Prepare bones and ground before Gift when practical, without delaying recovery. Review ordinary filler after readiness when the hold has no purpose. [1][10]

Ruby 3:20

Physical barrage

Steel Barrage and melee triggered Purgatory with nine bones. Leech cleared the debt; Death Strike and Gelopal's Lay on Hands followed. IBF was on cooldown.

Plan physical coverage and recovery resources before the barrage. AMS does not fix it. [1][19][20]

Den ~20:28

Bones vanish

Shred Armor, melee and Shoot drove you to 11.1% HP despite 101.2 RP. Gelopal's two Flash of Lights rescued you before your next Death Strike.

Rebuild bones and cover the transition. The cause of removal is unknown. [1]

Galvazzt 22:27

Forced tank soak

Galvanized meant 250% increased physical damage taken. Melee dealt 1.40M HP plus 339k absorbed; a beam tick left 11.9%. Gelopal healed before your Death Strike. All three DPS were dead.

Assign normal beams away from the tank. Forced soaks need physical coverage; AMS/AMZ do not fix amplified melee. [1][14][17][20][21]

Coordination, not blame

Call the backup.

64 direct Mind Freeze successes from 88 casts. Assign primary and backup kicks instead of blaming every overlap. Both Merektha knot rescues completed before Sleet, by 168 ms and 125 ms. These were backup overlaps, not failed rescues. Five Raise Ally casts produced five combat resurrections. [1][18]

Unfinished +17 / not a completed-key result

The final segment had no party deaths, and all five final observed HP samples were full. The log does not explain the ending. [1][15][16][20]

Build, comparisons and limits

Fourteen matching talent/gear snapshots support San'layn, full Dance of Midnight, four-piece, Heart 334, Tumor 321 and Maze-roa 334 with Sanguination. Ruby/Kings first expose the build at their first boss, not opening trash. Omnium ranks remain unproven. Peer routes, gear, support, add exposure and tuning may differ. SimC 12.1.0.69933 with October 3 hotfix data supplies selected priorities, not a personal simulation. No expected gain is claimed. [1][10][11]
