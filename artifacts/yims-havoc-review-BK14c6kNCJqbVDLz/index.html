01

Give Hunt a
beam to empower.

The Hunt grants a 12-second Empowered Eye Beam buff. It doubles the next beam's damage and widens its area. That is a different buff from Eternal Hunt's Blade Dance reset.1415

You received 23 empowerment applications. Eleven expired without a beam starting. That is the clearest recurring habit to change. Eleven unused buffs does not mean eleven avoidable mistakes, though. One Hunt landed only 285 milliseconds before Galvazzt ended.1

In the opening pull, Eye Beam started at 00:21.184, Hunt at 00:23.734, and the buff expired at 00:35.736. Abyssal Gaze arrived at 00:36.835, just 1.099 seconds late. The pack was still taking damage.1

The same ordering repeats on Adderis and Aspix. Beam at 03:29.303, Hunt at 03:31.865, expiry at 03:43.872, next beam at 03:50.259. On the large trash pull, Hunt at 06:16.658 follows Beam at 06:13.972, but the next beam waits until 06:36.641.1

Before Hunt, name the Beam/Gaze that will use it. Prefer a coming beam or a planned Meta reset.34 Start that beam while empowerment is active. It need not finish before the aura expires.15 You already get this right at 22:24.476 Hunt, then 22:24.898 Beam.1

Do not rush hard Meta past unspent Demonsurge spenders. Consume those opportunities and put Dance/Beam on cooldown first. If the reset sequence will not fit, pair Hunt with the pre-Meta beam instead. Do not hold a ready beam for a long wait on Hunt.34

02

Refresh Exergy.
Then burst.

Exergy grants 5% damage through Hunt and Vengeful Retreat. Watch its remaining time, not just Hunt's cooldown. Find a safe Retreat before Beam/Gaze on a long-lived target.27

Across the key, 93.76% of your damage occurred with Exergy present. Galvazzt drops to 75.80%. These are damage-presence shares, not combat uptime or a percentage of DPS lost.1

Two long gaps stand out. From 18:22.617 to 18:58.586, you dealt 4,173,491 damage to Galvazzt without Exergy, including Gaze, two Sweeps and another Beam. From 20:42.096 to 21:08.947, another 3,270,755 damage included Gaze and Essence Break's spender sequence.1

Both stretches contain repeated melee hits. The boss was available, but that does not make every Retreat direction safe. Keep soaking and frontal avoidance first. Plan the refresh and your return to melee, do not backflip blindly for a chart.12

All four peers kept at least 92.25% of their Galvazzt damage under Exergy. 双刃滑铲 reached 92.25%, Yogurtdip 100%.89 Dannedh's boss lasted almost as long as yours with similar spender counts. Vomitusmaxus took longer and had lower boss DPS, yet maintained the buff.11011 This is a maintenance lesson, not a boss-DPS ranking.

03

Font first.
Spenders next.

Font of Venomous Rage channels for two seconds. Prefer it with Initiative, before a fresh Demonic window and outside Essence Break, rather than spending that short window channeling.46

At 23:08.012, Beam opened demon form. Font began at 23:09.828, followed by Essence Break and two Sweeps. Demon form ended at 23:15.148. Chaos Strike followed at 23:15.231, with no Annihilation in the window. Font's resource snapshot showed 120/170 Fury.1

You already show the cleaner arrangement at 10:48.085 Retreat, 10:48.713 Font, 10:51.134 Beam. Yogurtdip uses the same ordering, then fits both Sweeps and Annihilation. Put the channel before fresh spender time when practical. An ending pack, brief target opportunity or already-spent long Meta can justify another order.149

A secondary Essence Break check

If Beam really has four or fewer seconds remaining, hold ordinary Essence Break until after it, unless an unspent Death Sweep or Annihilation Demonsurge opportunity takes priority. A visible shared Demonsurge aura is not the exception by itself.345

Merektha's 13:28.734 Essence Break precedes Beam by 4.705 seconds. Two Chaos Strikes use the debuff before the channel. This overlap is worth reviewing, not a proven four-second readiness violation or a guaranteed extra set of spenders.14
