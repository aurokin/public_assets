Main takeaway

Your broad cooldown habits are good[1][5][6]. The clearest personal damage fix is spending a ready Voidfall window before pressing another Fracture when you already have the resources. This is a button-ordering correction, not a recommendation to replace your build or use every defensive more aggressively[1][2][9].

I reviewed Roxysenpai, the report's only Demon Hunter, as Crunchy[1]. The main example is your Murder Row +16, with Kings' Rest +15, Ruby Life Pools +16, Altar of Fangs +15 and Kings' Rest +17 used to check the wider pattern[1]. The captured Murder Row build is Annihilator with all four Untethered Rage apex points[1].

1. When the meteor window is ready, stop building and spend it

In Murder Row, at 24:20.608, you pressed Fracture with six Soul Fragments, a ready Voidfall spending buff and fully capped Fury[1]. Warcraft Logs explicitly records all 28 Fury from that Fracture as wasted[1]. Your health snapshot was about 93%, and your Fracture hits show that an enemy was attackable in melee range[1].

Spirit Bomb was available[1][9]. Your previous Bomb was almost 49 seconds earlier[1]. You pressed it at 24:22.985, after that builder and a Flame sigil[1]. The Bomb still landed and spent the meteor window. This example proves delayed ordering and wasted Fury, not a lost meteor or a missing extra Bomb[1]. The current exact-build priority favors Bomb in that ready meteor window when you have enough souls[9]. Its special Fracture bridge is for needing Fury for the spender sequence, which you did not need here[9].

The easy hook is:

Ready Voidfall + enough souls + Spirit Bomb ready → spend the window before another full-resource Fracture[9].

A qualifying Fiery Brand can come ahead of Bomb, and needed defensives still take priority. This is not a rule that Bomb must always be your very next button[9]. If Bomb is unavailable, use Soul Cleave to spend Fury and souls as the window permits[2][9]. Keep room for the next generator instead of automatically repeating Fracture after every spender[2][9].

There is a similar example in the first Kings' Rest run at 6:00.931[1]. You pressed a second Fracture about one second after the previous one while holding six souls, lost its entire Fury gain, then used Soul Cleave[1]. You were at full health in that snapshot[1].

Murder Row records 3,518 wasted Fury out of 19,589 generated, about 18%, including 32 Fractures whose entire Fury gain was wasted[1]. Those are not 32 proven mistakes. Fracture still generates souls and Voidfall, and preventing charge cap can justify using it even when Fury is high[2][9]. The specific ready-window example is the correction; the whole-run waste is a reason to watch your ordering, not an 18% DPS-loss estimate.

2. Keep these habits

You are already using your important buttons[1]. In Murder Row you used 30 Fel Devastations, 35 Fiery Brands and 41 Sigils of Flame[1]. Three near-duration, near-gear Murder Row peers with your entire selected Annihilator hero package used 21 to 31, 30 to 34 and 35 to 41 respectively. That does not prove perfect timing, but there is no broad missing-cooldown habit here[5][6][7].

Your apex reaction is also good[1]. Every one of the 29 observed Untethered Rage procs in Murder Row was followed by a Meta activation before its aura disappeared[1]. Across the five completed keys, that was 158 out of 158[1]. This talent grants a temporary charge that you must press; you are already doing that[8]. Do not turn the review into "just press Meta more."

Your actual Meta-window spender pace was close to the matched peers[1][5][6]. The third matched peer supports the same conclusion[7]. Their whole-run Soul Cleave plus Spirit Bomb rate was moderately higher, but different full builds, routes and pull conditions prevent using that as a cast quota or promised DPS gain[5][6][7].

Next-key focus

Keep your cooldown and proc habits. For one key, watch Fury, soul count, Spirit Bomb readiness and the ready Voidfall spending buff together. The question before another Fracture is "Do I still need to build, or is my spender already set up?"

Do not force every Bomb to wait for six souls[9]. The current exact-build priority uses five normally, four during Meta and three while Fiery Demise is active[9]. You do not need to memorize those exceptions to fix the six-soul, full-Fury, ready-window example.

Survival still wins. You had no recorded deaths in the first four completed keys, then three in Kings' Rest +17[1]. I would not trade defensive coverage for cleaner damage alignment or prescribe a defensive overhaul from those counts alone.
