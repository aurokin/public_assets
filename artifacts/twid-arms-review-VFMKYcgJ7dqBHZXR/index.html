Main takeaway

Your damage cooldowns and core rotation are in good shape. The clearest repeatable cleanup is using your empowered Overpower sooner when Opportunist reaches two stacks, rather than leaving it capped while you continue the Execute/Mortal Strike loop[1][5]. This is a priority adjustment, not a recommendation to rebuild your character or force a cast quota.

I reviewed Twidw's Blinding Vale +19, fight 6, with the earlier incomplete Vale attempts kept separate[1]. Your captured build is Slayer with the complete Master of Warfare apex package, and all 76 logged talent rows passed the exact-build round-trip check[1].

1. Cash in two Opportunist stacks sooner

Opportunist comes from Tactician procs, which also reset Overpower. Its stacks empower your next Overpower[6]. When you reach two stacks, another proc can refresh the capped buff rather than giving you a larger bonus[1][6]. The useful screen cue is two Opportunist stacks, not an unexplained burst timer.

On Ziekett, Opportunist reached two stacks at 26:46.974. You kept it capped through two Mortal Strikes and several Executes before pressing Overpower at 26:54.630[1]. The buff received three further refreshes during that capped period[1]. Your boss damage continued throughout, and you were not locked in Bladestorm[1].

The Mortal Strikes at 26:47.449 and 26:51.424 correctly consumed two Executioner's Precision stacks[1]. Keep those, along with the Heroic Strike at 26:48.458[1][5]. The cleanup is the intervening paid Executes while that empowered Overpower was waiting. The exact Slayer execute priority puts two-stack Opportunist Overpower ahead of ordinary paid Execute, after the higher-priority two-stack Precision Mortal Strike[5].

This is not just one late-boss moment. On Lightwarden Ruia, you held two Opportunist stacks from 16:06.615, cast Mortal Strike, Execute and another Mortal Strike, then used Overpower at 16:14.029[1]. Those Mortal Strikes had only one Precision stack, and the same boss remained attackable throughout the sequence[1]. Both the normal single-target and execute priorities give the two-stack Opportunist Overpower an earlier place[5]. The Avatar/Smash pair at 16:10.451 / 16:10.465 remains an appropriate higher-priority burst action, not part of the criticism[1][5].

The next-key hook is:

On a single target, two Opportunist stacks → use the empowered Overpower before more ordinary filler[5].

Keep two-stack Precision Mortal Strike, a ready Heroic Strike proc and appropriate burst priorities ahead of it[5]. On real packs, Cleave still drives your AoE; this is not permission to replace ready Cleaves with Overpower indiscriminately[5].

You used 180 Overpowers in the key[1]. The near-duration, near-gear Slayer comparisons used 200 to 230[8][9][10]. That supports checking the habit, but their totals are not your target. The concrete boss sequences are the evidence for the correction, and I am not converting buff refreshes into an exact count of lost casts or a promised DPS gain.

Keep these habits

Your cooldown usage does not show a broad missing-button problem. You used 22 Avatars and 42 Colossus Smashes[1]. The matched peers used 20 to 23 Avatars and 41 to 45 Smashes, with broadly similar spending inside full Avatar windows[7][8][9]. The fourth peer supports the same conclusion[10]. I would not start this review with "just press your cooldowns more."

Your free-Execute handling is also sound. None of your 42 Smash casts began with two Sudden Death charges already held, leaving room for Tactical Edge's grant[1][6]. Of 185 observed Sudden Death buff periods, 181 contained an Execute before ending[1]. The four without one were two periods ending immediately before death and two procs near the end of boss kills, not a demonstrated habit of ignoring usable free Executes[1]. This is a buff-period check, not a claim that every individual proc charge was spent perfectly.

Do not let the raw Mortal Strike count mislead you. 198 of your 347 Mortal Strike events occurred inside Bladestorm, where Unhinged fires them automatically[1][6]. Your 149 outside-storm events were approximately equal to three of the four comparison players[8][9][10]. Aiwafury had higher outside-storm activity, but that one comparison does not establish that you should spam more Mortal Strike across the whole dungeon[7].

Your observed 231 Cleaves and 329 Executes are also within the comparison spread[1][8][9]. Keep the basic pack-cleave and free-Execute habits; improve the proc ordering rather than replacing the whole rotation.

Next-key focus

Make Opportunist and Executioner's Precision easy to read together. In single-target combat, ask whether you have an empowered Mortal Strike at two Precision stacks, an empowered Overpower at two Opportunist stacks, or only ordinary Execute/filler available[5].

For one key, focus on cashing in that two-stack Overpower before several more ordinary attacks. Do not hold a needed defensive, neglect a priority target or delay a useful burst window to obey a simplified priority list.

You had three recorded deaths in the completed key[1]. They matter, but this review does not turn them into unsupported missed-defensive accusations. The two proc-ordering examples above are living, actively attacking boss windows, not damage gaps caused by those deaths[1].
