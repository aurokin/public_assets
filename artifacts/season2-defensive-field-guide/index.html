Prevent it first

Prevent the extra damage first. A safe dispel, successful interrupt, rescue or dead totem changes the defensive assignment. Watch the cast and your own debuffs, not somebody else's route clock.[1][143][168][243][244][264]

Split the coverage

For a party hit, name one coverage owner and a recovery handoff. Give the selected bleed, DoT or mark holder a separate personal or external. Healers need self-protection too. Cover remaining ticks if selection arrives with the first hit; do not promise a warning that the log did not show.[56][57][114][212][282]

Keep the categories separate

Keep the categories separate. Rallying Cry adds temporary health. Holy Bulwark shields. Spirit Link combines local DR and health redistribution. Avenging Wrath boosts healing throughput. Healing Tide, healthstones and Lay on Hands restore HP. Those are different jobs, not interchangeable evidence of pre-hit DR.[28][93][98][99][102][170][201]

Sample, tuning and timing limits

This guide covers trash only, including named trash lieutenants. The comparison contains 30 distinct keys, 16 completed peer +23/+24 keys and 14 own +16 to +18 baselines. There are two peer teams per dungeon, not 16 globally independent teams. These are 25 decisions, not 25 routine overlaps.[56][58][131][133][203][206][207][208][306][308][310][311][60][135][136][137][138][202][204][205][209][312][313][314][315]

Own logs predate the October 13 tuning. Peer dates, compositions, routes and gear differ. Released changes checked through October 19 include Ruby's safe Living Bomb dispel, reduced damage in several dungeons, a longer Savage Leap cooldown and an eight-second Insidious Aura. Historical damage is not a current lethal threshold or fixed route timer.[1][103][60][135][138][312][314]

In every timed readout, zero is the first relevant observed non-tank damage in that window, not each holder's first hit. Minus means before it, plus means after it. Timings describe casts, not player intent, reaction speed, cooldown readiness or damage prevented. A cast alone does not establish active coverage, particularly for another player, an expired aura or a ground zone entered late.[56][104][212][266]

The named buttons are examples, not mandatory compositions. Base Feint covers AoE, not automatically a targeted bleed. Magic-only protection does not cover Physical Charge, Rattle, Echo or bleeds. Aura Mastery requires the appropriate aura and recipients; AMZ requires actual holder entry. Soulcoiler delivers capped DR, not shielding or healing, and can also trigger passively after an allied death.
