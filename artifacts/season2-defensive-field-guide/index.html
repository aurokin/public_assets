Match the damage

General reduction covers mixed Physical and magic damage. Magic-only protection misses Physical bleeds. Extra armor is not reduction against an armor-ignoring hit.

Short coverage belongs near impact. Use longer or staggered coverage for repeated ticks. Plan absorbs before damage and self-healing for recovery after surviving it.

01

Rav'i: Carrion Burst plus your meat soak

Assigned soaker, with party healing support. Planned soak.

Quick cue · Carrion Burst before your meat soak

When you see
Carrion Burst is ticking or you are unrecovered before your assigned meat soak.
Press
Longer Nature-capable or general reduction before entering. Cover the next ticks.
Still do
Catch your assigned meat, avoid unnecessary double soaks and break the shield. Plan group healing for the eating phase.
Full conditions & sources for Rav'i: Carrion Burst plus your meat soak

When Rav'i eats behind her shield, check your Carrion Burst before taking a Messy Eater circle. If already ticking or unrecovered, put a longer Nature-capable or general defensive up before entering the soak. Keep it through the next ticks. Catch the assigned meat, avoid unnecessary double soaks, and break the shield quickly. Plan a group healing cooldown for the eating phase[69][76][87].

02

Chieftain: Blood Sacrifice during totem pulses

Everyone. Mixed-pack priority.

Quick cue · Blood Sacrifice with totem pulses

When you see
Blood Sacrifice casts while an Unstable Totem pulses.
Press
General reduction before the Physical hit and healing absorb. Keep coverage for Nature pulses.
Still do
Plan group coverage if the party is low. Healing must clear the absorb before it can restore health.
Full conditions & sources for Chieftain: Blood Sacrifice during totem pulses

Blood Sacrifice casting while an Unstable Totem pulses is a pre-emptive defensive cue. Use general damage reduction before the Physical hit and healing absorb arrive, then cover the following Nature pulses. This is a good planned group cooldown when the party is already low. Ordinary healing must first clear the absorb, so waiting to react at low HP can leave recovery blocked[70][73][81].

04

Conditional: Rattle while Envenom is on you

Poisoned player. Conditional pack combination.

When you see
Envenom is on you when a Rattling Writhe casts Rattle. Conditional pack combination.
Press
Remove the poison where possible. Use general reduction before Rattle finishes and through its ticks.
Still do

Full conditions & sources for Conditional: Rattle while Envenom is on you

If your frame has Envenom when a Rattling Writhe casts Rattle, remove the poison where possible and use general damage reduction before the cast finishes. Cover Rattle's following ticks. Rattle is Physical and ignores armor; a magic-only button does not cover the whole overlap. This was not present in every sampled run, and it is not the Writhing Coil boss's Death Rattle[61][74][83].

On the other bosses, keep mechanic prevention separate. Break Death Rattle correctly as a group, interrupt Toxic Atrophy, and handle Zul'jan's beams and Ritual Venom cleanse assignments. The sample did not establish another recurring overlap worth inventing a cooldown rule for[46].
