When filler is right

Movement changes the choice. On Lithiel at 30:34.005 to 30:39.547, your position changes while Star sits at 40 souls. Devour can move with you; Star cannot. Do not stand in a mechanic to fix a meter.14

Keep the other exceptions. An instant Soulburst can take priority. Cleave may need Ray to restore Eradicate. Low Fury or an expiring Meta can require Ray or a soul pickup to keep a valuable spender possible. "Star first" applies to ordinary filler in a safe, ready state, not every cast regardless of resources.24

02

Notice when
burst is ready.

A conditional cue.
Not a cooldown-loss score.

Emptiness requires 50 stored entry souls. Track those separately from Star souls. Once entry is ready, ask whether you have a reason to hold before starting another outside-Meta generation cycle.2512

On Zaen, you reach 50 at 12:25.699 and enter Meta at 12:43.632, a 17.933-second hold. During it, you start Rays at 12:26.704 and 12:36.476, and finish five Consumes. These Ray timestamps are starts, not full-channel completions.1

This is a lower-confidence cue than the stationary Star examples. A mechanic or planned add wave can justify the hold. Keep a deliberate plan; stop the automatic extra cycle.12

The hold before Lithiel is reasonable. You reach 50 late in the preceding pull at 27:34.295, then enter at 28:04.560, just after Lithiel starts at 28:03.131. Do not turn this cue into "press Meta immediately everywhere."1

Zaen is a useful contrast. Illdiàn's 197.9-second pull has 13 completed Stars and 23 Ray starts; your 191.7-second pull has nine Stars and the same 23 starts.19 On Xathuux, you already finish more Stars than Rìners and Buterka.1710 The useful lesson is specific, not "every boss was bad."

Next key

Two cues.
That's it.

  1. Inside Meta: ready Star, healthy Fury, safe standing position. Spend Star before ordinary filler. Respect Soulburst, movement and survival exceptions.24
  2. Outside Meta: 50 entry souls. Enter unless you can name a short, deliberate reason to save the window.212

Finish the Rays you choose. Face the empowered Eradicate at useful targets. Use Devour for needed generation or required movement, not as a default after Star is ready. Review the exceptions after the key; do not chase peer totals mid-pull.24
